adapted from backdrop/internal/Shaders.kt under Apache-2.0. Modified for this
application: AGSL was translated to JS-generated SVG displacement data;
capsule-only geometry, bounded inward refraction, no dispersion/depth effect.
LiquidBottomTabs and its interaction utilities informed the component design.
This is a WebView adaptation, not the original native Compose implementation.
